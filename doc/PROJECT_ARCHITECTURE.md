PROJECT VISION

TURION AI Trader is not just another
Trading Bot.

The vision is to build a Professional
Artificial Intelligence Trading Platform
capable of understanding the market,
reasoning like an experienced trader,
learning from historical data,
and continuously improving its decisions.

The system should never depend on
a single indicator.

Every decision must be based on
multiple market factors working together.

The AI should always explain
why a decision was taken.

The primary objective is to protect capital,
manage risk,
and maximize long-term consistency.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Explainable

Every decision must include
a clear explanation.

Reliable

Never trade using incomplete information.

Modular

Every engine must work independently.

Scalable

New indicators,
strategies,
and AI models
should be added
without redesigning
the project.

Learning

The system must continuously improve
using historical data
and paper trading.

Human First

The AI should assist the trader,
not replace human judgment.
